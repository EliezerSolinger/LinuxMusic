# Problems and recovery

- The program saves a draft by itself and keeps a journal of the edits. After a crash, open the project: it offers to recover.
- Interrupted takes of a recording show up when the project opens, with Recover and Discard.
- Notices and activities: the notices icon in the tab bar. A new notice floats at the window's corner for a few seconds; a click closes it.
- Audio device gone: the notice offers Try again.
