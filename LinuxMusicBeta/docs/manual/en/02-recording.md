# Recording audio

1. Arm the audio track in its header and choose the input.
2. Press R (or the red button). With the count-in on, recording starts after the count-in bars.
3. Press R again while playing to punch in; again to punch out.
4. Stop: Space or Shift+Space. The take appears on the track and in Takes.

Count-in, pre-roll and the metronome are in the transport. The xruns counter shows the audio device's glitches; the CPU meter shows the audio load (red above 95 %).
