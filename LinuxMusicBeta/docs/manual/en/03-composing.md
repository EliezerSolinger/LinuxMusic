# Composing

The Composition page has the ruler, the tracks and the items.

- Tools: Draw (D), Knife (K), Select (V).
- Split at the cursor: S. Duplicate: Ctrl+D. Join: Ctrl+J. Delete: Delete.
- Marker: M. Region over the loop: Shift+R. Go to marker 1 to 9: keys 1 to 9.
- Enter opens the item's editor: Piano Roll (Ctrl+4), Automation (Ctrl+5) or Step editor (Ctrl+6).
- Zoom: = and -.

The project's time signature changes on the transport's signature button (N/D).
