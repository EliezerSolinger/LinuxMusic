# Mixing desk

The Mixing page has a channel per track, the returns and the master. The ANALOG / DIGITAL switch changes the kind of desk.

- Left and right arrows pick the channel; up and down move the fader (Shift: 3 dB, Ctrl: 0.1 dB).
- M and S: mute and solo. The transport's Master button cuts the whole output.
- The meters show each channel's peak; OUT in the transport shows the master.
- Inserts and sends appear on each channel; a click opens the effect's window.
